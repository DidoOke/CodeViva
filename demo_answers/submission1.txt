I changed it because with five retries and no waiting, a run where the fake failure rate hit a few jobs took ages in my tests, and it was just hammering the job again straight away. Three retries with exponential backoff gives a transient failure time to clear, but the trade-off is that a job that would have succeeded on the fifth try now fails.

With backoff capped at eight seconds, the worst case for one job is about 0.5 plus 1 plus 2 seconds of waiting, so a dependency that is down for a minute would make jobs fail rather than wait it out. That is a trade-off I accepted to keep runs short.
