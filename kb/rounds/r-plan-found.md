# Stated plan that was carried out

## Question
Last round you said: "{claim}". Your changes at {evidence_where} look like that work. How did the plan hold up once you started building it?

## Follow-up
Knowing what you know now, what would you have planned differently?

## Good answer mentions
- what turned out harder or different from what was expected
- whether the original reasoning still holds, or what changed it
- honest self-correction counts in the student's favour
