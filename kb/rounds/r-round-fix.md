# Bug fixed during this round

## Question
During this round you made commit {commit_short} ("{commit_message}"). Was that bug introduced by this round's changes, or was it already in your previous version?

## Follow-up
How did you confirm that the fix removes the problem?

## Good answer mentions
- traces the bug to a specific change (or shows it was latent)
- describes how it was reproduced or demonstrated
- a strong answer checks whether the bug could really happen, not only that the symptom went away
